1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a year of strong progress for Orchid Bay Hotels. Revenue reached about USD 950 million, up 12.9% on 2023, and our operating margin was 24.5%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Owned Hotels segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 17.8%. We continued to invest in our room nights and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We simplified our organisation and moved more of our support functions to shared service centres.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Orchid Bay Hotels Ltd. owns, manages and franchises hotels and serviced apartments for leisure travellers, corporate clients and event organisers. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Our balance sheet gives us the flexibility to invest through the cycle.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Victoria Moreau
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Orchid Bay Hotels Ltd. was founded in 1975 and owns, manages and franchises hotels and serviced apartments. We serve leisure travellers, corporate clients and event organisers in 46 countries. Our headquarters are in Hong Kong.

We report three segments: Owned Hotels, Managed and Franchised and Food and Beverage.

Owned Hotels. The Owned Hotels segment accounted for about 24% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Managed and Franchised. The Managed and Franchised segment accounted for about 43% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Food and Beverage. The Food and Beverage segment accounted for about 33% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Asia Pacific is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Europe, Middle East and Africa.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 9,600 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Acquisitions and integration

We have made, and may continue to make, acquisitions. We may not integrate acquired businesses successfully or realise the expected synergies, and we may need to record impairments of goodwill.

Travel demand

Hotel demand falls quickly in an economic downturn, a public-health emergency or after travel restrictions.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Property values

We own many of our hotels. A fall in property values could lead to impairment charges.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our room nights and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 12.9% to USD 945.6 million in 2024 from USD 837.3 million in 2023. Owned Hotels revenue grew 17.8%. Managed and Franchised revenue grew 15.2%. Food and Beverage revenue grew 7.0%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 48.4%, compared with 46.7% in 2023. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 24.0% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 231.3 million, and the operating margin was 24.5% (20.3% in 2023). Net income was USD 166.6 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 2.38.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 264.2 million. We invested USD 238.9 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 42.6 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 205.8 million and total borrowings were USD 262.4 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
