1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2025 was a year of steady progress for Orchid Bay Hotels. Revenue reached about USD 990 million, up 4.7% on 2024, and our operating margin was 25.0%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Managed and Franchised segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 9.2%. We continued to invest in our room nights and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We simplified our organisation and moved more of our support functions to shared service centres.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Orchid Bay Hotels Ltd. owns, manages and franchises hotels and serviced apartments for leisure travellers, corporate clients and event organisers. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. We will keep returning surplus cash to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Victoria Moreau
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Orchid Bay Hotels Ltd. was founded in 1975 and owns, manages and franchises hotels and serviced apartments. We serve leisure travellers, corporate clients and event organisers in 46 countries. Our headquarters are in Hong Kong.

We report three segments: Owned Hotels, Managed and Franchised and Food and Beverage.

Owned Hotels. The Owned Hotels segment accounted for about 22% of revenue in 2025. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Managed and Franchised. The Managed and Franchised segment accounted for about 44% of revenue in 2025. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Food and Beverage. The Food and Beverage segment accounted for about 34% of revenue in 2025. It has been our fastest-growing business over the last three years.

Asia Pacific is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Europe, Middle East and Africa.

At 31 December 2025, we employed approximately 11,400 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

Property values

We own many of our hotels. A fall in property values could lead to impairment charges.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Cybersecurity and data protection

Our systems and those of our partners are targets of cyberattacks. A successful attack could disrupt our operations, expose personal data and lead to regulatory investigations and claims.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Acquisitions and integration

We have made, and may continue to make, acquisitions. We may not integrate acquired businesses successfully or realise the expected synergies, and we may need to record impairments of goodwill.

Travel demand

Hotel demand falls quickly in an economic downturn, a public-health emergency or after travel restrictions.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2025 with 2024. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 4.7% to USD 990.3 million in 2025 from USD 945.6 million in 2024. Owned Hotels revenue fell 6.5%. Managed and Franchised revenue grew 9.2%. Food and Beverage revenue grew 7.1%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 48.3%, compared with 48.4% in 2024. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 23.3% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 247.3 million, and the operating margin was 25.0% (24.5% in 2024). Net income was USD 187.0 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 2.69.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 344.5 million. We invested USD 150.8 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 34.8 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 203.5 million and total borrowings were USD 236.3 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2026 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
