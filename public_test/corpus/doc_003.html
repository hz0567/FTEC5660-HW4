1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a challenging year for Brightwater Energy. Revenue reached USD 2.3 billion, up 0.2% on 2023, and our operating margin was 22.8%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Midstream segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 8.9%. We continued to invest in our output and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We opened new facilities and expanded our sales teams in several markets.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Brightwater Energy Corp. produces natural gas and oil, operates pipelines and develops wind and solar projects for utilities, refiners and industrial users. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Our balance sheet gives us the flexibility to invest through the cycle.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Ursula Zhang
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Brightwater Energy Corp. was founded in 2002 and produces natural gas and oil, operates pipelines and develops wind and solar projects. We serve utilities, refiners and industrial users in 35 countries. Our headquarters are in Calgary.

We report three segments: Upstream, Midstream and Renewables.

Upstream. The Upstream segment accounted for about 22% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Midstream. The Midstream segment accounted for about 29% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Renewables. The Renewables segment accounted for about 48% of revenue in 2024. It has been our fastest-growing business over the last three years.

Europe, Middle East and Africa is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 2,500 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our output and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Commodity price volatility

Our revenue depends on oil and gas prices, which can move sharply because of global supply, weather and geopolitical events.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Energy transition

Policies that reduce demand for fossil fuels could shorten the economic life of some of our assets and lead to impairments.

Climate and physical risks

Extreme weather can damage our facilities and those of our suppliers and customers. New climate regulation may increase our energy and compliance costs.

Acquisitions and integration

We have made, and may continue to make, acquisitions. We may not integrate acquired businesses successfully or realise the expected synergies, and we may need to record impairments of goodwill.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 0.2% to USD 2,252.2 million in 2024 from USD 2,246.7 million in 2023. Upstream revenue fell 4.5%. Midstream revenue grew 8.9%. Renewables revenue fell 2.2%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 32.2%, compared with 32.6% in 2023. Research and development expenses were 1.1% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 8.3% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 514.1 million, and the operating margin was 22.8% (23.4% in 2023). Net income was USD 369.1 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 2.95.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 666.1 million. We invested USD 409.3 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 139.7 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 303.8 million and total borrowings were USD 332.0 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
