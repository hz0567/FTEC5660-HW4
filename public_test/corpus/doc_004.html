1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2025 was a year of steady progress for Sablewood Retail. Revenue reached USD 2.4 billion, up 2.5% on 2024, and our operating margin was 8.5%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Wholesale segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 6.7%. We continued to invest in our products and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We opened new facilities and expanded our sales teams in several markets.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Sablewood Retail Inc. sells apparel, footwear and home goods through stores, its website and wholesale partners for consumers and department-store partners. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Our balance sheet gives us the flexibility to invest through the cycle.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Olivia Petrov
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Sablewood Retail Inc. was founded in 1953 and sells apparel, footwear and home goods through stores, its website and wholesale partners. We serve consumers and department-store partners in 36 countries. Our headquarters are in Columbus.

We report three segments: Stores, E-commerce and Wholesale.

Stores. The Stores segment accounted for about 39% of revenue in 2025. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

E-commerce. The E-commerce segment accounted for about 25% of revenue in 2025. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Wholesale. The Wholesale segment accounted for about 36% of revenue in 2025. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Americas is our largest region. We also have operations in Europe, Middle East and Africa and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2025, we employed approximately 14,100 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

Acquisitions and integration

We have made, and may continue to make, acquisitions. We may not integrate acquired businesses successfully or realise the expected synergies, and we may need to record impairments of goodwill.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our products and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Consumer spending

Our sales depend on discretionary consumer spending, which falls when employment, wages or consumer confidence weaken.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Climate and physical risks

Extreme weather can damage our facilities and those of our suppliers and customers. New climate regulation may increase our energy and compliance costs.

Inventory and markdowns

We order most products months before the season. If we misjudge demand, we must sell excess inventory at lower prices, which reduces our gross margin.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2025 with 2024. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 2.5% to USD 2,390.3 million in 2025 from USD 2,332.4 million in 2024. Stores revenue fell 0.9%. E-commerce revenue grew 2.2%. Wholesale revenue grew 6.7%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 40.4%, compared with 40.0% in 2024. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 31.9% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 204.2 million, and the operating margin was 8.5% (9.0% in 2024). Net income was USD 151.7 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 3.69.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 183.9 million. We invested USD 81.9 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 24.3 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 529.7 million and total borrowings were USD 436.5 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2026 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
