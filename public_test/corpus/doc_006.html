Brightwater Energy Corp. (TSX: BWE)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 3 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Liam Hoffmann - Head of Investor Relations
  • Ursula Zhang - Chief Executive Officer
  • Oscar Silva - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Ivan Xu - Harbourline Securities
  • Ethan Tanaka - Kowloon Peak Securities
  • Fiona Vasquez - Meridian Equity Research

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Brightwater Energy Corp. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Liam Hoffmann.

Liam Hoffmann (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Ursula Zhang, our Chief Executive Officer, and Oscar Silva, our Chief Financial Officer.

Ursula Zhang (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Liam, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 586 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 2.4 billion, up about 8% year on year. Renewables was again our strongest segment. Customer demand improved through the year, and our order book entering 2026 is healthy.

Ursula Zhang (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Oscar for the financial details.

Oscar Silva (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 32.0% and operating margin was 23.3%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 850 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Oscar Silva (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 200 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Oscar Silva (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 12.5%, and an operating margin of 22 to 24 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ivan Xu with Harbourline Securities.

Ivan Xu (Harbourline Securities)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Oscar Silva (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ethan Tanaka with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Ethan Tanaka (Kowloon Peak Securities)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Ursula Zhang (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Fiona Vasquez with Meridian Equity Research.

Fiona Vasquez (Meridian Equity Research)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Oscar Silva (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ivan Xu with Harbourline Securities.

Ivan Xu (Harbourline Securities)

Can you give more colour on Upstream?

Ursula Zhang (Chief Executive Officer)

Upstream performed in line with our expectations. We see a good pipeline, although some customers are taking longer to make decisions.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
