Sablewood Retail Inc. (NYSE: SBWR)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 5 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Gabriel Novak - Head of Investor Relations
  • Olivia Petrov - Chief Executive Officer
  • Isabel Petrov - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Kenji Umeh - BuyFin Capital
  • Priya Hoffmann - Kowloon Peak Securities
  • Qing Ruiz - Harbourline Securities

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Sablewood Retail Inc. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Gabriel Novak.

Gabriel Novak (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Olivia Petrov, our Chief Executive Officer, and Isabel Petrov, our Chief Financial Officer.

Olivia Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Gabriel, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 621 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 2.4 billion, up about 2% year on year. Wholesale was again our strongest segment. We gained share in several of our core markets.

Olivia Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Isabel for the financial details.

Isabel Petrov (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 40.4% and operating margin was 8.5%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 180 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Isabel Petrov (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 75 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Isabel Petrov (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 8.5%, and an operating margin of 8 to 10 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Kenji Umeh with BuyFin Capital.

Kenji Umeh (BuyFin Capital)

Could you talk about pricing? Are you still able to pass on cost increases to customers?

Olivia Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Pricing remained constructive. We implemented targeted increases at the start of the year, and retention has been good. We do not expect the same level of price increases in 2026.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Priya Hoffmann with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Priya Hoffmann (Kowloon Peak Securities)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Isabel Petrov (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Qing Ruiz with Harbourline Securities.

Qing Ruiz (Harbourline Securities)

Can you give more colour on Stores?

Olivia Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Stores performed in line with our expectations. We see a good pipeline, although some customers are taking longer to make decisions.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Kenji Umeh with BuyFin Capital.

Kenji Umeh (BuyFin Capital)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Olivia Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
