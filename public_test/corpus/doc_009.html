Corvane Pharma Ltd. (NASDAQ: CRVP)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 7 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Ana Bianchi - Head of Investor Relations
  • Gabriel Petrov - Chief Executive Officer
  • Thomas Moreau - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Ursula Abbott - Harbourline Securities
  • Kavya Brennan - Meridian Equity Research
  • Adrian Sato - Kowloon Peak Securities

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Corvane Pharma Ltd. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Ana Bianchi.

Ana Bianchi (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Gabriel Petrov, our Chief Executive Officer, and Thomas Moreau, our Chief Financial Officer.

Gabriel Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Ana, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 750 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 3.1 billion, up about 15% year on year. Oncology was again our strongest segment. The year started slowly, but activity picked up in the second half.

Gabriel Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Thomas for the financial details.

Thomas Moreau (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 68.2% and operating margin was 20.5%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 490 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Thomas Moreau (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 150 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Thomas Moreau (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 8.5%, and an operating margin of 19 to 21 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ursula Abbott with Harbourline Securities.

Ursula Abbott (Harbourline Securities)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Gabriel Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Kavya Brennan with Meridian Equity Research.

Kavya Brennan (Meridian Equity Research)

Could you talk about pricing? Are you still able to pass on cost increases to customers?

Gabriel Petrov (Chief Executive Officer)

Pricing remained constructive. We implemented targeted increases at the start of the year, and retention has been good. We do not expect the same level of price increases in 2026.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Adrian Sato with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Adrian Sato (Kowloon Peak Securities)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Thomas Moreau (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ursula Abbott with Harbourline Securities.

Ursula Abbott (Harbourline Securities)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Thomas Moreau (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
