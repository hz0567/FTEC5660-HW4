Sablewood Retail Inc. (SBWR)

FY2025 results review: solid year, but priced in

7 March 2026 | Analyst: Grace Dubois

  • Rating: HOLD
  • 12-month target price: USD 66.36
  • Share price (close, 6 March 2026): USD 64.17
  • Implied upside: 3.4%

Investment summary

Sablewood Retail Inc. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 2,390.3 million (+2.5% year on year) and operating income of USD 204.2 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 8.5% for FY2026. We reiterate our HOLD rating with a target price of USD 66.36.

The results were solid, but we think the current share price already reflects the improvement in margins. We would become more positive on evidence of faster growth.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 8.5% in FY2025, versus 9.0% in FY2024.
  • Stores and E-commerce remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We raise our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 2,605.8 million from USD 2,476.9 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 4.13.

Risks to our view

  • Consumer spending: Our sales depend on discretionary consumer spending, which falls when employment, wages or consumer confidence weaken.
  • Inventory and markdowns: We order most products months before the season. If we misjudge demand, we must sell excess inventory at lower prices, which reduces our gross margin.
