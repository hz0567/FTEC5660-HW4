Northgate Payments Corp. (NGPY)

FY2025 results review: growth slowing, valuation stretched

21 March 2026 | Analyst: Xin Tanaka

  • Rating: REDUCE
  • 12-month target price: USD 49.41
  • Share price (close, 20 March 2026): USD 52.84
  • Implied upside: -6.5%

Investment summary

Northgate Payments Corp. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 1,333.6 million (+15.0% year on year) and operating income of USD 280.5 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 7.5% for FY2026. We move to our REDUCE rating with a target price of USD 49.41.

We see downside risk to consensus estimates as growth slows, and the shares trade at a premium to peers.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 21.0% in FY2025, versus 20.4% in FY2024.
  • Merchant Acquiring and Issuer Processing remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We cut our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 1,426.0 million from USD 1,448.7 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 2.40.

Risks to our view

  • Payment network rules and fees: We rely on card networks and real-time payment schemes. Changes to network rules, interchange caps or scheme fees could reduce the margin we earn on each transaction.
  • Fraud and chargebacks: We bear losses when merchants cannot cover chargebacks. A rise in card-not-present fraud would increase our transaction losses and could damage our relationships with banks.
