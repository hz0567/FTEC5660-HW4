Northgate Robotics Inc. (NGR)

FY2025 results review: solid year, but priced in

9 March 2026 | Analyst: Hannah Yilmaz

  • Rating: HOLD
  • 12-month target price: USD 72.99
  • Share price (close, 8 March 2026): USD 66.84
  • Implied upside: 9.2%

Investment summary

Northgate Robotics Inc. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 2,710.4 million (+3.1% year on year) and operating income of USD 353.1 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 10% for FY2026. We move to our HOLD rating with a target price of USD 72.99.

The results were solid, but we think the current share price already reflects the improvement in margins. We would become more positive on evidence of faster growth.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 13.0% in FY2025, versus 11.7% in FY2024.
  • Factory Automation and Collaborative Robots remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We raise our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 2,945.7 million from USD 2,850.6 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 2.57.

Risks to our view

  • Cyclical demand for capital equipment: Demand for capital equipment is cyclical. When manufacturers postpone factory investments, orders for our robots fall quickly, and our order backlog may not protect revenue for more than two quarters.
  • Dependence on component suppliers: We depend on a small number of suppliers for precision gearboxes and servo motors. A disruption at one of these suppliers could delay shipments and increase our costs.
