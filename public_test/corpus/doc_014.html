Northgate Robotics Inc. (NYSE: NGR)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 5 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Chloe Schultz - Head of Investor Relations
  • Liam Ferreira - Chief Executive Officer
  • Thomas Kaur - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Ethan Yilmaz - Kowloon Peak Securities
  • Beatrice Chen - BuyFin Capital
  • William Osei - Meridian Equity Research

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Northgate Robotics Inc. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Chloe Schultz.

Chloe Schultz (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Liam Ferreira, our Chief Executive Officer, and Thomas Kaur, our Chief Financial Officer.

Liam Ferreira (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Chloe, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 665 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 2.7 billion, up about 3% year on year. Collaborative Robots was again our strongest segment. The year started slowly, but activity picked up in the second half.

Liam Ferreira (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Thomas for the financial details.

Thomas Kaur (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 35.0% and operating margin was 13.0%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 340 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Thomas Kaur (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 125 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Thomas Kaur (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 10%, and an operating margin of 12 to 14 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ethan Yilmaz with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Ethan Yilmaz (Kowloon Peak Securities)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Liam Ferreira (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Beatrice Chen with BuyFin Capital.

Beatrice Chen (BuyFin Capital)

Could you talk about pricing? Are you still able to pass on cost increases to customers?

Liam Ferreira (Chief Executive Officer)

Pricing remained constructive. We implemented targeted increases at the start of the year, and retention has been good. We do not expect the same level of price increases in 2026.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from William Osei with Meridian Equity Research.

William Osei (Meridian Equity Research)

Can you give more colour on Services and Software?

Liam Ferreira (Chief Executive Officer)

Services and Software performed in line with our expectations. We see a good pipeline, although some customers are taking longer to make decisions.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ethan Yilmaz with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Ethan Yilmaz (Kowloon Peak Securities)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Thomas Kaur (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
