Lumen Harbor Foods plc (LHF)

FY2025 results review: momentum intact, upside remains

5 March 2026 | Analyst: Liam Rossi

  • Rating: BUY
  • 12-month target price: USD 75.76
  • Share price (close, 4 March 2026): USD 65.34
  • Implied upside: 15.9%

Investment summary

Lumen Harbor Foods plc reported FY2025 revenue of USD 1,848.5 million (+1.6% year on year) and operating income of USD 274.7 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 14% for FY2026. We reiterate our BUY rating with a target price of USD 75.76.

We believe the market underestimates the durability of the company's growth and its ability to expand margins. The new share repurchase programme adds support.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 14.9% in FY2025, versus 15.0% in FY2024.
  • Bakery and Dairy and Chilled remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We cut our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 2,129.5 million from USD 2,234.8 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 5.67.

Risks to our view

  • Commodity prices: Wheat, dairy, sugar and packaging account for most of our cost of revenue. We cannot always pass sudden increases in commodity prices on to retailers.
  • Retailer concentration: A few supermarket chains account for a large part of our sales, and they can negotiate lower prices, longer payment terms or more shelf-space fees.
