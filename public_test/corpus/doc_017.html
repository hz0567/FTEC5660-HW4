Kestrel Semiconductor Ltd. (KSTL)

FY2025 results review: growth slowing, valuation stretched

14 March 2026 | Analyst: Connor Haddad

  • Rating: REDUCE
  • 12-month target price: USD 61.16
  • Share price (close, 13 March 2026): USD 67.36
  • Implied upside: -9.2%

Investment summary

Kestrel Semiconductor Ltd. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 2,409.4 million (+10.7% year on year) and operating income of USD 695.0 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 9% for FY2026. We reiterate our REDUCE rating with a target price of USD 61.16.

We see downside risk to consensus estimates as growth slows, and the shares trade at a premium to peers.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 28.8% in FY2025, versus 27.9% in FY2024.
  • Automotive Chips and Data Center remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We raise our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 2,617.5 million from USD 2,555.1 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 5.13.

Risks to our view

  • Industry cycles: The semiconductor industry is cyclical. In a downturn, customers reduce orders and draw down their inventories, and our fabs run below capacity.
  • Export controls: Export-control rules may restrict sales of some of our products to certain customers or countries, or the use of some equipment in our fabs.
