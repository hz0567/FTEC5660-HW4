1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a year of steady progress for Sablewood Retail. Revenue reached USD 2.3 billion, up 3.7% on 2023, and our operating margin was 9.0%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Stores segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 7.3%. We continued to invest in our products and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We simplified our organisation and moved more of our support functions to shared service centres.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Sablewood Retail Inc. sells apparel, footwear and home goods through stores, its website and wholesale partners for consumers and department-store partners. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. We will keep returning surplus cash to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Olivia Petrov
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Sablewood Retail Inc. was founded in 1953 and sells apparel, footwear and home goods through stores, its website and wholesale partners. We serve consumers and department-store partners in 36 countries. Our headquarters are in Columbus.

We report three segments: Stores, E-commerce and Wholesale.

Stores. The Stores segment accounted for about 41% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

E-commerce. The E-commerce segment accounted for about 25% of revenue in 2024. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Wholesale. The Wholesale segment accounted for about 34% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Americas is our largest region. We also have operations in Europe, Middle East and Africa and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 13,400 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

Inventory and markdowns

We order most products months before the season. If we misjudge demand, we must sell excess inventory at lower prices, which reduces our gross margin.

Taxation

We operate in many tax jurisdictions. Changes in tax laws, including the global minimum tax, or disputes with tax authorities could increase our effective tax rate.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Consumer spending

Our sales depend on discretionary consumer spending, which falls when employment, wages or consumer confidence weaken.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Acquisitions and integration

We have made, and may continue to make, acquisitions. We may not integrate acquired businesses successfully or realise the expected synergies, and we may need to record impairments of goodwill.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 3.7% to USD 2,332.4 million in 2024 from USD 2,250.0 million in 2023. Stores revenue grew 7.3%. E-commerce revenue grew 0.9%. Wholesale revenue grew 1.5%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 40.0%, compared with 39.9% in 2023. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 30.9% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 210.8 million, and the operating margin was 9.0% (9.2% in 2023). Net income was USD 150.2 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 3.61.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 306.0 million. We invested USD 83.8 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 53.8 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 507.2 million and total borrowings were USD 516.4 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
