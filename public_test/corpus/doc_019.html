Brightwater Energy Corp. (BWE)

FY2025 results review: momentum intact, upside remains

9 March 2026 | Analyst: Ursula Kowalski

  • Rating: BUY
  • 12-month target price: USD 82.62
  • Share price (close, 8 March 2026): USD 65.45
  • Implied upside: 26.2%

Investment summary

Brightwater Energy Corp. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 2,436.3 million (+8.2% year on year) and operating income of USD 566.7 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 12.5% for FY2026. We reiterate our BUY rating with a target price of USD 82.62.

We believe the market underestimates the durability of the company's growth and its ability to expand margins. The new share repurchase programme adds support.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 23.3% in FY2025, versus 22.8% in FY2024.
  • Upstream and Midstream remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We cut our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 2,754.0 million from USD 2,802.4 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 3.85.

Risks to our view

  • Commodity price volatility: Our revenue depends on oil and gas prices, which can move sharply because of global supply, weather and geopolitical events.
  • Energy transition: Policies that reduce demand for fossil fuels could shorten the economic life of some of our assets and lead to impairments.
