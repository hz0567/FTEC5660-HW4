1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2025 was a year of strong progress for Brightwater Software. Revenue reached USD 1.6 billion, up 24.3% on 2024, and our operating margin was 18.8%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Professional Services segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 28.2%. We continued to invest in our subscriptions and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We simplified our organisation and moved more of our support functions to shared service centres.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Brightwater Software Inc. sells cloud software for finance, procurement and supply-chain teams for mid-sized and large enterprises. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Our balance sheet gives us the flexibility to invest through the cycle.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Yara Castillo
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Brightwater Software Inc. was founded in 1995 and sells cloud software for finance, procurement and supply-chain teams. We serve mid-sized and large enterprises in 54 countries. Our headquarters are in Austin.

We report three segments: Cloud Subscriptions, Licenses and Professional Services.

Cloud Subscriptions. The Cloud Subscriptions segment accounted for about 27% of revenue in 2025. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Licenses. The Licenses segment accounted for about 34% of revenue in 2025. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Professional Services. The Professional Services segment accounted for about 39% of revenue in 2025. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Europe, Middle East and Africa is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2025, we employed approximately 4,800 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Taxation

We operate in many tax jurisdictions. Changes in tax laws, including the global minimum tax, or disputes with tax authorities could increase our effective tax rate.

Hosting and outages

We host our software with third-party cloud providers. A prolonged outage could breach our service-level commitments and lead to credits or lost customers.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Customer retention

Our growth depends on customers renewing and expanding their subscriptions. If renewal rates fall, our revenue would grow more slowly than our costs.

Cybersecurity and data protection

Our systems and those of our partners are targets of cyberattacks. A successful attack could disrupt our operations, expose personal data and lead to regulatory investigations and claims.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2025 with 2024. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 24.3% to USD 1,574.7 million in 2025 from USD 1,266.8 million in 2024. Cloud Subscriptions revenue grew 20.5%. Licenses revenue grew 23.1%. Professional Services revenue grew 28.2%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 76.0%, compared with 76.4% in 2024. Research and development expenses were 22.4% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 34.7% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 296.7 million, and the operating margin was 18.8% (20.5% in 2024). Net income was USD 235.5 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 3.25.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 233.0 million. We invested USD 31.5 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 54.0 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 330.7 million and total borrowings were USD 318.1 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2026 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
