Brightwater Software Inc. (BWSW)

FY2025 results review: solid year, but priced in

23 March 2026 | Analyst: Laura Moreau

  • Rating: HOLD
  • 12-month target price: USD 79.34
  • Share price (close, 22 March 2026): USD 80.23
  • Implied upside: -1.1%

Investment summary

Brightwater Software Inc. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 1,574.7 million (+24.3% year on year) and operating income of USD 296.7 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 11.5% for FY2026. We reiterate our HOLD rating with a target price of USD 79.34.

The results were solid, but we think the current share price already reflects the improvement in margins. We would become more positive on evidence of faster growth.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 18.8% in FY2025, versus 20.5% in FY2024.
  • Cloud Subscriptions and Licenses remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We cut our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 1,736.5 million from USD 1,770.1 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 3.67.

Risks to our view

  • Customer retention: Our growth depends on customers renewing and expanding their subscriptions. If renewal rates fall, our revenue would grow more slowly than our costs.
  • Hosting and outages: We host our software with third-party cloud providers. A prolonged outage could breach our service-level commitments and lead to credits or lost customers.
