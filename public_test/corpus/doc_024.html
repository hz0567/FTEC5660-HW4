1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a year of strong progress for Corvane Pharma. Revenue reached USD 2.6 billion, up 14.4% on 2023, and our operating margin was 19.3%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Established Products segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 20.0%. We continued to invest in our medicines and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We completed a multi-year upgrade of our core systems.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Corvane Pharma Ltd. discovers, develops and markets prescription medicines for hospitals, specialty pharmacies and wholesalers. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Our balance sheet gives us the flexibility to invest through the cycle.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Gabriel Petrov
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Corvane Pharma Ltd. was founded in 1975 and discovers, develops and markets prescription medicines. We serve hospitals, specialty pharmacies and wholesalers in 12 countries. Our headquarters are in Boston.

We report three segments: Oncology, Immunology and Established Products.

Oncology. The Oncology segment accounted for about 33% of revenue in 2024. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Immunology. The Immunology segment accounted for about 36% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Established Products. The Established Products segment accounted for about 31% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Americas is our largest region. We also have operations in Europe, Middle East and Africa and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 5,000 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our medicines and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Clinical and regulatory risk

Most product candidates fail in clinical trials. Even after approval, regulators may restrict the label of a medicine or require additional studies, which delays launches.

Climate and physical risks

Extreme weather can damage our facilities and those of our suppliers and customers. New climate regulation may increase our energy and compliance costs.

Patent expiries

When the patents protecting a medicine expire, generic or biosimilar competitors usually enter the market and prices fall sharply.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

Taxation

We operate in many tax jurisdictions. Changes in tax laws, including the global minimum tax, or disputes with tax authorities could increase our effective tax rate.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 14.4% to USD 2,649.8 million in 2024 from USD 2,316.8 million in 2023. Oncology revenue grew 13.3%. Immunology revenue grew 10.8%. Established Products revenue grew 20.0%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 68.1%, compared with 68.6% in 2023. Research and development expenses were 20.8% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 28.0% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 512.1 million, and the operating margin was 19.3% (23.5% in 2023). Net income was USD 383.5 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 4.36.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 368.1 million. We invested USD 128.5 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 83.8 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 546.8 million and total borrowings were USD 440.3 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
