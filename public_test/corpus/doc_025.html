Northgate Payments Corp. (NASDAQ: NGPY)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 3 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Gabriel Park - Head of Investor Relations
  • Leon Nyberg - Chief Executive Officer
  • Priya Vasquez - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Thomas Okafor - Meridian Equity Research
  • Farid Kaur - BuyFin Capital
  • Vikram Tran - Harbourline Securities

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Northgate Payments Corp. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Gabriel Park.

Gabriel Park (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Leon Nyberg, our Chief Executive Officer, and Priya Vasquez, our Chief Financial Officer.

Leon Nyberg (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Gabriel, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 323 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 1.3 billion, up about 15% year on year. Merchant Acquiring was again our strongest segment. We gained share in several of our core markets.

Leon Nyberg (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Priya for the financial details.

Priya Vasquez (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 55.4% and operating margin was 21.0%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 220 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Priya Vasquez (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 75 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Priya Vasquez (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 7.5%, and an operating margin of 20 to 22 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Thomas Okafor with Meridian Equity Research.

Thomas Okafor (Meridian Equity Research)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Priya Vasquez (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Farid Kaur with BuyFin Capital.

Farid Kaur (BuyFin Capital)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Leon Nyberg (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Vikram Tran with Harbourline Securities.

Vikram Tran (Harbourline Securities)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Priya Vasquez (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Thomas Okafor with Meridian Equity Research.

Thomas Okafor (Meridian Equity Research)

Can you give more colour on Issuer Processing?

Leon Nyberg (Chief Executive Officer)

Issuer Processing performed in line with our expectations. We see a good pipeline, although some customers are taking longer to make decisions.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
