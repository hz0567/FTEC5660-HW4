1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2025 was a year of strong progress for Kestrel Semiconductor. Revenue reached USD 2.4 billion, up 10.7% on 2024, and our operating margin was 28.8%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Data Center segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 16.0%. We continued to invest in our chips and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We opened new facilities and expanded our sales teams in several markets.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Kestrel Semiconductor Ltd. designs and manufactures power-management and connectivity chips for automotive suppliers, server makers and consumer-electronics brands. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Capital discipline remains central to how we allocate resources.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Isabel Xu
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Kestrel Semiconductor Ltd. was founded in 1966 and designs and manufactures power-management and connectivity chips. We serve automotive suppliers, server makers and consumer-electronics brands in 55 countries. Our headquarters are in Hsinchu.

We report three segments: Automotive Chips, Data Center and Consumer Devices.

Automotive Chips. The Automotive Chips segment accounted for about 27% of revenue in 2025. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Data Center. The Data Center segment accounted for about 38% of revenue in 2025. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Consumer Devices. The Consumer Devices segment accounted for about 34% of revenue in 2025. It has been our fastest-growing business over the last three years.

Europe, Middle East and Africa is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2025, we employed approximately 5,400 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

Industry cycles

The semiconductor industry is cyclical. In a downturn, customers reduce orders and draw down their inventories, and our fabs run below capacity.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Climate and physical risks

Extreme weather can damage our facilities and those of our suppliers and customers. New climate regulation may increase our energy and compliance costs.

Taxation

We operate in many tax jurisdictions. Changes in tax laws, including the global minimum tax, or disputes with tax authorities could increase our effective tax rate.

Export controls

Export-control rules may restrict sales of some of our products to certain customers or countries, or the use of some equipment in our fabs.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our chips and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Acquisitions and integration

We have made, and may continue to make, acquisitions. We may not integrate acquired businesses successfully or realise the expected synergies, and we may need to record impairments of goodwill.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2025 with 2024. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 10.7% to USD 2,409.4 million in 2025 from USD 2,175.8 million in 2024. Automotive Chips revenue grew 9.3%. Data Center revenue grew 16.0%. Consumer Devices revenue grew 6.5%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 53.2%, compared with 52.4% in 2024. Research and development expenses were 15.5% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 8.9% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 695.0 million, and the operating margin was 28.8% (27.9% in 2024). Net income was USD 528.9 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 4.64.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 725.0 million. We invested USD 302.6 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 194.7 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 495.3 million and total borrowings were USD 1,070.7 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2026 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
