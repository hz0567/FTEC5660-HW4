1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a year of strong progress for Brightwater Software. Revenue reached USD 1.3 billion, up 22.0% on 2023, and our operating margin was 20.5%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Cloud Subscriptions segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 27.3%. We continued to invest in our subscriptions and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We simplified our organisation and moved more of our support functions to shared service centres.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Brightwater Software Inc. sells cloud software for finance, procurement and supply-chain teams for mid-sized and large enterprises. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Capital discipline remains central to how we allocate resources.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Yara Castillo
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Brightwater Software Inc. was founded in 1995 and sells cloud software for finance, procurement and supply-chain teams. We serve mid-sized and large enterprises in 54 countries. Our headquarters are in Austin.

We report three segments: Cloud Subscriptions, Licenses and Professional Services.

Cloud Subscriptions. The Cloud Subscriptions segment accounted for about 28% of revenue in 2024. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Licenses. The Licenses segment accounted for about 35% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Professional Services. The Professional Services segment accounted for about 38% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Europe, Middle East and Africa is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 3,500 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Hosting and outages

We host our software with third-party cloud providers. A prolonged outage could breach our service-level commitments and lead to credits or lost customers.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Customer retention

Our growth depends on customers renewing and expanding their subscriptions. If renewal rates fall, our revenue would grow more slowly than our costs.

Cybersecurity and data protection

Our systems and those of our partners are targets of cyberattacks. A successful attack could disrupt our operations, expose personal data and lead to regulatory investigations and claims.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our subscriptions and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 22.0% to USD 1,266.8 million in 2024 from USD 1,038.4 million in 2023. Cloud Subscriptions revenue grew 27.3%. Licenses revenue grew 13.9%. Professional Services revenue grew 26.4%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 76.4%, compared with 77.1% in 2023. Research and development expenses were 23.5% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 32.3% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 260.1 million, and the operating margin was 20.5% (20.5% in 2023). Net income was USD 200.3 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 2.73.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 183.6 million. We invested USD 30.9 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 46.4 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 244.9 million and total borrowings were USD 286.1 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
