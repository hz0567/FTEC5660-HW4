1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a year of steady progress for Northgate Robotics. Revenue reached USD 2.6 billion, up 5.1% on 2023, and our operating margin was 11.7%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Services and Software segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 6.0%. We continued to invest in our robots and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We opened new facilities and expanded our sales teams in several markets.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Northgate Robotics Inc. designs and manufactures robotic arms, motion controllers and machine-vision systems for automotive, electronics and food-processing manufacturers. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Capital discipline remains central to how we allocate resources.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Liam Ferreira
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Northgate Robotics Inc. was founded in 1981 and designs and manufactures robotic arms, motion controllers and machine-vision systems. We serve automotive, electronics and food-processing manufacturers in 26 countries. Our headquarters are in Pittsburgh.

We report three segments: Factory Automation, Collaborative Robots and Services and Software.

Factory Automation. The Factory Automation segment accounted for about 35% of revenue in 2024. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Collaborative Robots. The Collaborative Robots segment accounted for about 40% of revenue in 2024. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Services and Software. The Services and Software segment accounted for about 25% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Americas is our largest region. We also have operations in Europe, Middle East and Africa and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 8,100 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Cyclical demand for capital equipment

Demand for capital equipment is cyclical. When manufacturers postpone factory investments, orders for our robots fall quickly, and our order backlog may not protect revenue for more than two quarters.

Dependence on component suppliers

We depend on a small number of suppliers for precision gearboxes and servo motors. A disruption at one of these suppliers could delay shipments and increase our costs.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our robots and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Cybersecurity and data protection

Our systems and those of our partners are targets of cyberattacks. A successful attack could disrupt our operations, expose personal data and lead to regulatory investigations and claims.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 5.1% to USD 2,629.1 million in 2024 from USD 2,501.7 million in 2023. Factory Automation revenue grew 5.8%. Collaborative Robots revenue grew 4.0%. Services and Software revenue grew 6.0%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 34.5%, compared with 35.5% in 2023. Research and development expenses were 7.2% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 15.6% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 307.6 million, and the operating margin was 11.7% (12.1% in 2023). Net income was USD 201.9 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 1.86.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 250.7 million. We invested USD 136.3 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 76.2 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 215.7 million and total borrowings were USD 946.8 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
