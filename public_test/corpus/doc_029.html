Kestrel Semiconductor Ltd. (NASDAQ: KSTL)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 24 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Nadia Osei - Head of Investor Relations
  • Isabel Xu - Chief Executive Officer
  • Leon Haddad - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Elena Larsen - Harbourline Securities
  • Rafael Ferreira - Kowloon Peak Securities
  • Ivan Kowalski - BuyFin Capital

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Kestrel Semiconductor Ltd. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Nadia Osei.

Nadia Osei (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Isabel Xu, our Chief Executive Officer, and Leon Haddad, our Chief Financial Officer.

Isabel Xu (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Nadia, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 599 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 2.4 billion, up about 11% year on year. Data Center was again our strongest segment. Customer demand improved through the year, and our order book entering 2026 is healthy.

Isabel Xu (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Leon for the financial details.

Leon Haddad (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 53.2% and operating margin was 28.8%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 720 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Leon Haddad (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 375 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Leon Haddad (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 9%, and an operating margin of 28 to 30 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Elena Larsen with Harbourline Securities.

Elena Larsen (Harbourline Securities)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Isabel Xu (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Rafael Ferreira with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Rafael Ferreira (Kowloon Peak Securities)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Leon Haddad (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Ivan Kowalski with BuyFin Capital.

Ivan Kowalski (BuyFin Capital)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Leon Haddad (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Elena Larsen with Harbourline Securities.

Elena Larsen (Harbourline Securities)

Could you talk about pricing? Are you still able to pass on cost increases to customers?

Isabel Xu (Chief Executive Officer)

Pricing remained constructive. We implemented targeted increases at the start of the year, and retention has been good. We do not expect the same level of price increases in 2026.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
