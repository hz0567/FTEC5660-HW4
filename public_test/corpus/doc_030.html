Halcyon Freight Inc. (NYSE: HFRT)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 21 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Hiroshi Moreau - Head of Investor Relations
  • James Kaur - Chief Executive Officer
  • Rachel Nyberg - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Beatrice O'Neill - Kowloon Peak Securities
  • Daniel Dubois - Meridian Equity Research
  • Kavya Hoffmann - BuyFin Capital

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Halcyon Freight Inc. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Hiroshi Moreau.

Hiroshi Moreau (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are James Kaur, our Chief Executive Officer, and Rachel Nyberg, our Chief Financial Officer.

James Kaur (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Hiroshi, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 478 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 1.9 billion, up about 3% year on year. Intermodal was again our strongest segment. The year started slowly, but activity picked up in the second half.

James Kaur (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Rachel for the financial details.

Rachel Nyberg (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 24.7% and operating margin was 13.9%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 300 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Rachel Nyberg (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 100 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Rachel Nyberg (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 14%, and an operating margin of 13 to 15 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Beatrice O'Neill with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Beatrice O'Neill (Kowloon Peak Securities)

Can you give more colour on Contract Logistics?

James Kaur (Chief Executive Officer)

Contract Logistics performed in line with our expectations. We see a good pipeline, although some customers are taking longer to make decisions.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Daniel Dubois with Meridian Equity Research.

Daniel Dubois (Meridian Equity Research)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Rachel Nyberg (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Kavya Hoffmann with BuyFin Capital.

Kavya Hoffmann (BuyFin Capital)

Could you talk about pricing? Are you still able to pass on cost increases to customers?

James Kaur (Chief Executive Officer)

Pricing remained constructive. We implemented targeted increases at the start of the year, and retention has been good. We do not expect the same level of price increases in 2026.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Beatrice O'Neill with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Beatrice O'Neill (Kowloon Peak Securities)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Rachel Nyberg (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
