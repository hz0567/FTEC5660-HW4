1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2025 was a year of strong progress for Corvane Pharma. Revenue reached USD 3.1 billion, up 15.2% on 2024, and our operating margin was 20.5%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Oncology segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 20.9%. We continued to invest in our medicines and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We simplified our organisation and moved more of our support functions to shared service centres.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Corvane Pharma Ltd. discovers, develops and markets prescription medicines for hospitals, specialty pharmacies and wholesalers. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Capital discipline remains central to how we allocate resources.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Gabriel Petrov
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Corvane Pharma Ltd. was founded in 1975 and discovers, develops and markets prescription medicines. We serve hospitals, specialty pharmacies and wholesalers in 12 countries. Our headquarters are in Boston.

We report three segments: Oncology, Immunology and Established Products.

Oncology. The Oncology segment accounted for about 35% of revenue in 2025. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Immunology. The Immunology segment accounted for about 33% of revenue in 2025. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Established Products. The Established Products segment accounted for about 32% of revenue in 2025. It has been our fastest-growing business over the last three years.

Americas is our largest region. We also have operations in Europe, Middle East and Africa and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2025, we employed approximately 6,400 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Interest rates and financing

We borrow under bank facilities and bonds. Higher interest rates increase our finance costs, and a deterioration of credit markets could make it harder to refinance our borrowings when they mature.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Patent expiries

When the patents protecting a medicine expire, generic or biosimilar competitors usually enter the market and prices fall sharply.

Clinical and regulatory risk

Most product candidates fail in clinical trials. Even after approval, regulators may restrict the label of a medicine or require additional studies, which delays launches.

Taxation

We operate in many tax jurisdictions. Changes in tax laws, including the global minimum tax, or disputes with tax authorities could increase our effective tax rate.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2025 with 2024. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 15.2% to USD 3,053.4 million in 2025 from USD 2,649.8 million in 2024. Oncology revenue grew 20.9%. Immunology revenue grew 6.6%. Established Products revenue grew 19.1%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 68.2%, compared with 68.1% in 2024. Research and development expenses were 20.0% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 27.7% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 625.7 million, and the operating margin was 20.5% (19.3% in 2024). Net income was USD 474.0 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 5.44.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 487.9 million. We invested USD 119.8 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 151.6 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 579.9 million and total borrowings were USD 469.0 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2026 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
