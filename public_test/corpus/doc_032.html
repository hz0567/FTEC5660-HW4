1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2025 was a year of strong progress for Brightwater Energy. Revenue reached USD 2.4 billion, up 8.2% on 2024, and our operating margin was 23.3%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Renewables segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 11.4%. We continued to invest in our output and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We opened new facilities and expanded our sales teams in several markets.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Brightwater Energy Corp. produces natural gas and oil, operates pipelines and develops wind and solar projects for utilities, refiners and industrial users. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Capital discipline remains central to how we allocate resources.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Ursula Zhang
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Brightwater Energy Corp. was founded in 2002 and produces natural gas and oil, operates pipelines and develops wind and solar projects. We serve utilities, refiners and industrial users in 35 countries. Our headquarters are in Calgary.

We report three segments: Upstream, Midstream and Renewables.

Upstream. The Upstream segment accounted for about 21% of revenue in 2025. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Midstream. The Midstream segment accounted for about 29% of revenue in 2025. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Renewables. The Renewables segment accounted for about 50% of revenue in 2025. Its revenue is partly seasonal, with a stronger second half.

Europe, Middle East and Africa is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Asia Pacific.

At 31 December 2025, we employed approximately 2,600 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Commodity price volatility

Our revenue depends on oil and gas prices, which can move sharply because of global supply, weather and geopolitical events.

Foreign exchange

We report in US dollars, but a significant part of our revenue and costs is in other currencies. Exchange-rate movements affect our reported results and the value of our foreign assets.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Climate and physical risks

Extreme weather can damage our facilities and those of our suppliers and customers. New climate regulation may increase our energy and compliance costs.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

Cybersecurity and data protection

Our systems and those of our partners are targets of cyberattacks. A successful attack could disrupt our operations, expose personal data and lead to regulatory investigations and claims.

Energy transition

Policies that reduce demand for fossil fuels could shorten the economic life of some of our assets and lead to impairments.

Taxation

We operate in many tax jurisdictions. Changes in tax laws, including the global minimum tax, or disputes with tax authorities could increase our effective tax rate.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2025 with 2024. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 8.2% to USD 2,436.3 million in 2025 from USD 2,252.2 million in 2024. Upstream revenue grew 0.5%. Midstream revenue grew 8.7%. Renewables revenue grew 11.4%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 32.0%, compared with 32.2% in 2024. Research and development expenses were 1.0% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 7.8% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 566.7 million, and the operating margin was 23.3% (22.8% in 2024). Net income was USD 415.4 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 3.35.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 845.7 million. We invested USD 397.5 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 66.9 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 363.2 million and total borrowings were USD 350.1 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2026 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
