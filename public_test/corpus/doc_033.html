Corvane Pharma Ltd. (CRVP)

FY2025 results review: growth slowing, valuation stretched

21 March 2026 | Analyst: Hannah Quinn

  • Rating: REDUCE
  • 12-month target price: USD 152.28
  • Share price (close, 20 March 2026): USD 162.99
  • Implied upside: -6.6%

Investment summary

Corvane Pharma Ltd. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 3,053.4 million (+15.2% year on year) and operating income of USD 625.7 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 8.5% for FY2026. We reiterate our REDUCE rating with a target price of USD 152.28.

We see downside risk to consensus estimates as growth slows, and the shares trade at a premium to peers.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 20.5% in FY2025, versus 19.3% in FY2024.
  • Oncology and Immunology remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We cut our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 3,332.6 million from USD 3,399.1 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 5.94.

Risks to our view

  • Clinical and regulatory risk: Most product candidates fail in clinical trials. Even after approval, regulators may restrict the label of a medicine or require additional studies, which delays launches.
  • Patent expiries: When the patents protecting a medicine expire, generic or biosimilar competitors usually enter the market and prices fall sharply.
