Brightwater Software Inc. (NASDAQ: BWSW)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 19 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Ana Lindqvist - Head of Investor Relations
  • Yara Castillo - Chief Executive Officer
  • Rachel Maddox - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Pedro Lambert - Harbourline Securities
  • Connor Tanaka - BuyFin Capital
  • Thomas Abbott - Kowloon Peak Securities

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Brightwater Software Inc. fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Ana Lindqvist.

Ana Lindqvist (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Yara Castillo, our Chief Executive Officer, and Rachel Maddox, our Chief Financial Officer.

Yara Castillo (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Ana, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 419 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 1.6 billion, up about 24% year on year. Professional Services was again our strongest segment. We gained share in several of our core markets.

Yara Castillo (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Rachel for the financial details.

Rachel Maddox (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 76.0% and operating margin was 18.8%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 230 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Rachel Maddox (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 75 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Rachel Maddox (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 11.5%, and an operating margin of 18 to 20 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Pedro Lambert with Harbourline Securities.

Pedro Lambert (Harbourline Securities)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Rachel Maddox (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Connor Tanaka with BuyFin Capital.

Connor Tanaka (BuyFin Capital)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Rachel Maddox (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Thomas Abbott with Kowloon Peak Securities.

Thomas Abbott (Kowloon Peak Securities)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Yara Castillo (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Pedro Lambert with Harbourline Securities.

Pedro Lambert (Harbourline Securities)

Can you give more colour on Cloud Subscriptions?

Yara Castillo (Chief Executive Officer)

Cloud Subscriptions performed in line with our expectations. We see a good pipeline, although some customers are taking longer to make decisions.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
