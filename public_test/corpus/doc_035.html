Halcyon Freight Inc. (HFRT)

FY2025 results review: growth slowing, valuation stretched

5 March 2026 | Analyst: Vikram O'Neill

  • Rating: REDUCE
  • 12-month target price: USD 139.84
  • Share price (close, 4 March 2026): USD 153.19
  • Implied upside: -8.7%

Investment summary

Halcyon Freight Inc. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 1,911.3 million (+2.6% year on year) and operating income of USD 266.4 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 14% for FY2026. We reiterate our REDUCE rating with a target price of USD 139.84.

We see downside risk to consensus estimates as growth slows, and the shares trade at a premium to peers.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 13.9% in FY2025, versus 13.9% in FY2024.
  • Truckload and Intermodal remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We raise our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 2,203.8 million from USD 2,148.3 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 7.76.

Risks to our view

  • Fuel prices: Fuel is one of our largest operating costs. Fuel surcharges recover most, but not all, of an increase in diesel prices, and they recover it with a delay.
  • Driver availability: The industry faces a shortage of qualified truck drivers. Higher wages and recruiting costs may reduce our margins, and a lack of drivers may limit our capacity.
