1. Letter to shareholders

Dear shareholders,

2024 was a year of steady progress for Lumen Harbor Foods. Revenue reached USD 1.8 billion, up 6.5% on 2023, and our operating margin was 15.0%. I want to thank our employees, customers and partners for their commitment throughout the year.

Our Dairy and Chilled segment was the main driver of growth, with revenue up 14.2%. We continued to invest in our products and in the digital tools our customers use every day, while keeping a close eye on costs. We opened new facilities and expanded our sales teams in several markets.

Our strategy remains unchanged. Lumen Harbor Foods plc produces and sells branded and private-label packaged foods for supermarket chains, convenience stores and food-service distributors. We want to be the partner of choice in each of our markets, to grow faster than our industry and to convert a high share of our earnings into cash. Capital discipline remains central to how we allocate resources.

Looking ahead, we see both opportunities and risks. Demand in some of our end markets remains uncertain, and inflation in wages and materials has not fully eased. We will stay focused on what we can control: serving our customers well, improving productivity and developing our people.

Sincerely,
Qing Abbott
Chief Executive Officer

2. Business overview

Lumen Harbor Foods plc was founded in 1961 and produces and sells branded and private-label packaged foods. We serve supermarket chains, convenience stores and food-service distributors in 51 countries. Our headquarters are in Manchester.

We report three segments: Bakery, Dairy and Chilled and Snacks.

Bakery. The Bakery segment accounted for about 35% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Dairy and Chilled. The Dairy and Chilled segment accounted for about 33% of revenue in 2024. Its customers typically sign multi-year agreements.

Snacks. The Snacks segment accounted for about 32% of revenue in 2024. It competes mainly on quality, reliability and service.

Asia Pacific is our largest region. We also have operations in Americas and Europe, Middle East and Africa.

At 31 December 2024, we employed approximately 7,700 people.

3. Risk factors

The risks below could materially affect our business, financial condition and results. They are not the only risks we face.

Climate and physical risks

Extreme weather can damage our facilities and those of our suppliers and customers. New climate regulation may increase our energy and compliance costs.

Competition

We compete with large international groups and with smaller specialists. Competitors may introduce better or cheaper offerings, which could reduce our market share and our margins.

Litigation

From time to time we are involved in legal proceedings, including commercial disputes and employment claims. An unfavourable outcome could require us to pay damages or change how we operate.

Commodity prices

Wheat, dairy, sugar and packaging account for most of our cost of revenue. We cannot always pass sudden increases in commodity prices on to retailers.

Talent

Our success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees, especially engineers and experienced managers. Competition for talent is intense in all our main markets.

Regulation and compliance

We are subject to many laws, including competition, anti-corruption, sanctions, tax and data-protection rules. Changes in these laws, or a failure to comply with them, could result in penalties and restrictions on our business.

Macroeconomic conditions

A slowdown in the economies where we operate, high inflation or a recession could reduce demand for our products and put pressure on our prices. We may not be able to reduce our costs as quickly as our revenue falls.

Retailer concentration

A few supermarket chains account for a large part of our sales, and they can negotiate lower prices, longer payment terms or more shelf-space fees.

4. Management's discussion and analysis

Overview

The following discussion compares our results for 2024 with 2023. All amounts are in USD millions unless stated otherwise, and percentages are calculated on unrounded figures.

Revenue

Revenue increased 6.5% to USD 1,819.4 million in 2024 from USD 1,708.3 million in 2023. Bakery revenue grew 2.9%. Dairy and Chilled revenue grew 14.2%. Snacks revenue grew 3.4%.

Gross margin and operating expenses

Gross margin was 33.0%, compared with 31.0% in 2023. Research and development expenses were 1.0% of revenue. Selling, general and administrative expenses were 16.9% of revenue.

Operating income and net income

Operating income was USD 273.6 million, and the operating margin was 15.0% (11.9% in 2023). Net income was USD 187.7 million and diluted earnings per share were USD 4.44.

Liquidity and capital resources

Net cash from operating activities was USD 274.6 million. We invested USD 152.2 million in property, plant and equipment and paid dividends of USD 31.8 million. At year end, cash and cash equivalents were USD 445.1 million and total borrowings were USD 372.8 million.

Outlook

We expect market conditions in 2025 to remain mixed. We plan to keep investing in our priority segments while managing costs carefully. Our guidance for the coming year is published separately with our fourth-quarter results.
