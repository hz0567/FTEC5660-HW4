Orchid Bay Hotels Ltd. (1876)

FY2025 results review: solid year, but priced in

9 March 2026 | Analyst: Zara Okafor

  • Rating: HOLD
  • 12-month target price: USD 79.02
  • Share price (close, 8 March 2026): USD 77.48
  • Implied upside: 2.0%

Investment summary

Orchid Bay Hotels Ltd. reported FY2025 revenue of USD 990.3 million (+4.7% year on year) and operating income of USD 247.3 million. Management guided to revenue growth of approximately 11.5% for FY2026. We move to our HOLD rating with a target price of USD 79.02.

The results were solid, but we think the current share price already reflects the improvement in margins. We would become more positive on evidence of faster growth.

Key takeaways

  • Operating margin of 25.0% in FY2025, versus 24.5% in FY2024.
  • Owned Hotels and Managed and Franchised remain the core of the investment case.
  • Balance sheet remains conservative; capital returns are increasing.

Estimate changes

We raise our FY2026E revenue estimate to USD 1,096.4 million from USD 1,043.7 million, and our FY2026E diluted EPS estimate to USD 3.03.

Risks to our view

  • Travel demand: Hotel demand falls quickly in an economic downturn, a public-health emergency or after travel restrictions.
  • Property values: We own many of our hotels. A fall in property values could lead to impairment charges.
