Lumen Harbor Foods plc (LSE: LHF)

Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

Date: 20 February 2026

Call participants

Company participants

  • Priya Abbott - Head of Investor Relations
  • Qing Abbott - Chief Executive Officer
  • Hiroshi Brennan - Chief Financial Officer

Analysts

  • Nadia Yilmaz - Meridian Equity Research
  • Benjamin Kowalski - BuyFin Capital
  • Nikolai Thornton - Harbourline Securities

Presentation

Operator (Operator)

Good morning, and welcome to the Lumen Harbor Foods plc fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings conference call. All participants are in listen-only mode. I would now like to hand the call over to Priya Abbott.

Priya Abbott (Head of Investor Relations)

Thank you, operator. Today's remarks include forward-looking statements, which are subject to risks described in our annual report. We will also refer to non-GAAP measures; reconciliations are available on our website. With me today are Qing Abbott, our Chief Executive Officer, and Hiroshi Brennan, our Chief Financial Officer.

Qing Abbott (Chief Executive Officer)

Thank you, Priya, and good morning, everyone. We closed 2025 with fourth-quarter revenue of about USD 477 million. For the full year, revenue was USD 1.8 billion, up about 2% year on year. Bakery was again our strongest segment. Customer demand improved through the year, and our order book entering 2026 is healthy.

Qing Abbott (Chief Executive Officer)

We also made progress on our strategic priorities. We launched new offerings, improved our service levels and continued to simplify the organisation. I am proud of what our teams achieved, and I will now hand over to Hiroshi for the financial details.

Hiroshi Brennan (Chief Financial Officer)

Thank you. Gross margin for the year was 31.8% and operating margin was 14.9%. Operating cash flow was strong at about USD 310 million. We ended the year with a solid balance sheet.

Hiroshi Brennan (Chief Financial Officer)

Turning to capital allocation, the board has approved a new share repurchase programme of up to USD 100 million, to be executed over the next 18 months, in addition to our regular dividend.

Hiroshi Brennan (Chief Financial Officer)

Now to our outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of approximately 14%, and an operating margin of 14 to 16 percent. This assumes no major change in exchange rates or in the macroeconomic environment.

Question and answer session

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Nadia Yilmaz with Meridian Equity Research.

Nadia Yilmaz (Meridian Equity Research)

Are you considering acquisitions, or is the buyback the priority?

Hiroshi Brennan (Chief Financial Officer)

Our first priority is organic investment. We will look at bolt-on acquisitions that fit our strategy, but we will remain disciplined on price. The buyback reflects our confidence in the business.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Benjamin Kowalski with BuyFin Capital.

Benjamin Kowalski (BuyFin Capital)

How should we think about the margin bridge into 2026?

Hiroshi Brennan (Chief Financial Officer)

Mix and productivity should be tailwinds, partly offset by wage inflation and continued investment in our growth initiatives. That is reflected in the range we gave.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Nikolai Thornton with Harbourline Securities.

Nikolai Thornton (Harbourline Securities)

Could you talk about pricing? Are you still able to pass on cost increases to customers?

Qing Abbott (Chief Executive Officer)

Pricing remained constructive. We implemented targeted increases at the start of the year, and retention has been good. We do not expect the same level of price increases in 2026.

Operator (Operator)

The next question comes from Nadia Yilmaz with Meridian Equity Research.

Nadia Yilmaz (Meridian Equity Research)

What are you seeing in terms of hiring and attrition?

Qing Abbott (Chief Executive Officer)

Attrition came down during the year, and we were able to fill most of our key roles. We continue to invest in training and in our leadership pipeline.

Operator (Operator)

That concludes our question and answer session. Thank you for joining, and you may now disconnect.
